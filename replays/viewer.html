    How our bot decides each move

    It never sees the opponent's cards. Every decision runs the same six steps in a fraction of a second, and the panel above shows step 5 for each move.

    1. Track the hand

      Replays every action to know the street, the bets, the chips each side has in and who is all-in.

      _apply
    2. Read the opponent

      Counts how often they fold, call, raise and go all-in in 8 different spots, starting from sensible guesses; labels their style.

      stats · profile()
    3. Narrow their range

      Weights all 1,326 hands they could hold. Each action keeps the hands that would act that way.

      _update_range
    4. Simulate

      Deals out up to 3,000 random finishes against that weighted range to get our equity; exact on the river.

      _equity
    5. Score every option

      Expected value of fold, call, each bet size and all-in. Fold chance comes from how strong their range is, corrected by what our past bluffs against them did.

      _raise_ev · _weak_share
    6. Act, then learn

      Plays the best option. After the hand it updates the opponent counts and its bluff record.

      _decide · end_game
